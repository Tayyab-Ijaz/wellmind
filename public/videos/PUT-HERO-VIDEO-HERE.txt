WELLMIND HERO VIDEO
===================

Put your hero video in THIS folder with this exact filename:

    hero.mp4

The website will automatically load it at:
    /videos/hero.mp4

Recommended source quality:
- 3840 x 2160 (4K) for maximum detail
- 30 or 60 FPS
- H.264 MP4 for broad browser compatibility
- Keep the original high-quality export; do not upload a low-resolution preview
- Landscape 16:9 works best for the full-screen hero

The hero video is muted, autoplaying, looping and plays inline. Muting is required
for reliable autoplay in modern browsers. There is no CSS blur, canvas resizing,
or image filter applied to the video.
